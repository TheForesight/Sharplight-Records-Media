Our Mission

At Sharplight Records Media, we believe creativity knows no bounds. Our mission is to deliver comprehensive media production services—from stunning videos and immersive audio to expert music and instrument classes—that empower individuals and businesses to express their visions and thrive in the dynamic world of media and art.


Our Vision

At Sharplight Records Media, we envision a future where media creativity is accessible to all. We aim to be the go-to hub for comprehensive media solutions and education, bridging technology with artistry to empower global communities, foster inclusive storytelling, and redefine how the world experiences media.


Our Values

We're dedicated to sustainability, ethical practices, and nurturing local talent across all media forms. By supporting diverse creators with top-tier production and hands-on training, we create positive impacts in the industry, ensuring every project delivers exceptional experiences that inspire and engage audiences.


Our Story

Founded in 2016 as a passion-driven venture, Sharplight Records Media has blossomed into a versatile media powerhouse. From intimate music sessions to full-scale productions and skill-building classes, we've helped countless creators turn ideas into reality, always prioritizing quality, innovation, and community in everything we do.
